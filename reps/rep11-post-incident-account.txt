REP 11 — POST-INCIDENT ACCOUNT

I am accountable for allowing the fictional disk-remediation workflow to
increase the data volume to 2000 Gi without human approval.
The diagnosis was right but I took an autonomy configuration that had a very expensive infrastructure
change that I could leave it as an ACT action. 
The alert cleared and VERIFY said that there was 65 percent available space, 
but the workflow was an unplanned $1,840 per-month cost, 
so I believe it's a governance failure. I will put higher level actions like storage 
provisioning, deletion, service stops and irreversible cleanup behind the approval gate 
as the current platform on call engineer will approve each of the actions I mentioned. 
I will also cap autonomous storage growth at 100Gi per incident and have a $100 Estimated Monthly
Recurring Cost (EMRC) cap. If either limit is exceeded it will stop and page a human. 
The runbook rule will be: "An agent is not permitted to resize a volume or insert recurring cost without named (human) approval 
in audit.log. I will test the block, approval, verification and budget paths prior to enabling the workflow.
