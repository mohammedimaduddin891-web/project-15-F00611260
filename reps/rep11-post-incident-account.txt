REP 11 — POST-INCIDENT ACCOUNT

This section is a personal accountability judgment. Rewrite it fully in your
own voice before submission.

I am accountable for allowing the fictional disk-remediation workflow to resize
the data volume to 2000 Gi without human approval. The diagnosis of low free
space was correct, but I accepted an autonomy policy that treated a costly
infrastructure change as an unattended ACT action. The alert cleared, yet the
workflow created an unplanned $1,840 monthly expense, so the overall outcome
was a governance failure. I will require the approval gate for storage
provisioning, deletion, service-stop, and irreversible cleanup actions. The
current platform on-call engineer must approve one named action, and the token
will expire after fifteen minutes. I will also enforce a maximum autonomous
storage increase of 100 Gi per incident and a $100 estimated monthly cost cap;
anything above either limit will stop and page a human. The runbook will state:
"An agent must never resize a volume or create recurring cost without named
human approval recorded in audit.log." I will test the block, approval, and
budget paths before enabling the workflow.
