REP 4 — AUTONOMY LADDER

Action                                      Tier       Justification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
du -x -d1 /var | sort -rh                   ACT        Read-only, no state change,
                                                        and limited blast radius.

kubectl rollout restart deploy/api          APPROVE    State-changing and usually
                                                        reversible, but it can cause
                                                        availability impact.

journalctl --vacuum-size=500M                APPROVE    Deletes logs permanently even
                                                        though the host blast radius is
                                                        low.

resize-pvc data-var --to 500Gi               APPROVE    Changes infrastructure cost and
                                                        may be difficult to reverse.

systemctl stop nginx                         SUGGEST    Directly removes service
                                                        availability; a human should
                                                        decide and perform or explicitly
                                                        approve it under a change record.

apt-get clean                                APPROVE    Cache removal is recoverable, but
                                                        it changes state and can affect
                                                        later package installation during
                                                        an incident.

Reflection draft — paraphrase this

I used the same two questions for every action: whether the action is reversible
and how large its blast radius is. A teammate might place apt-get clean at ACT
because its effect is reversible when packages are downloaded again. I keep it
at APPROVE in this runbook because automatic cache deletion during an incident
could delay an urgent rollback or package reinstall, while the cost of waiting
for approval is small.
