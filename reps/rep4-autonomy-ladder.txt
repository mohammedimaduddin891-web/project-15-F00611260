REP 4 — AUTONOMY LADDER

Action                                      Tier       Justification
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
du -x -d1 /var | sort -rh                   ACT        Read-only, no state change,
                                                        and very small blast radius.
kubectl rollout restart deploy/api          APPROVE    State-changing and usually
                                                        reversible, but can affect
                                                        service availability.
journalctl --vacuum-size=500M               APPROVE    Permanently removes old logs;
                                                        host blast radius is limited.
resize-pvc data-var --to 500Gi              APPROVE    Changes infrastructure capacity
                                                        and recurring cost and may be
                                                        difficult to reverse.
systemctl stop nginx                        SUGGEST    Directly removes service
                                                        availability; a human should
                                                        decide under change control.
apt-get clean                               APPROVE    Recoverable, but changes state
                                                        and could delay an urgent
                                                        reinstall or rollback.

Reflection
For each action, I asked about whether the action was reversible and about the size of the blast radius.
In terms of availability, data and cost. The apt-get clean command could be considered a reasonable teammate.
As ACT – downloadable again, but I put it in APPROVE as
the cost of waiting for approval is small and automatic clean-up during incident.
May hamper recuperation.
