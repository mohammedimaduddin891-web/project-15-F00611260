Reflection draft — paraphrase this

The required HUMAN_APPROVAL_TOKEN value is I-APPROVE. Keeping the destructive
pattern and requiring a separate approval token is safer than deleting the
pattern because the default remains deny-by-default; the exception is explicit,
auditable, and limited to the approved execution rather than permanently
weakening the policy.
