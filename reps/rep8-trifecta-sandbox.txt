Reflection draft — paraphrase this

This is an architectural fix because the corrected agent no longer possesses
all three capabilities at the same time. A prompt filter can miss obfuscated,
encoded, multilingual, or newly phrased instructions and can also be bypassed
by trusted-looking telemetry. Removing or gating the outbound tool makes
exfiltration impossible even when the model interprets the hostile text as an
instruction. The safety property therefore comes from capability design rather
than confidence in text filtering.
