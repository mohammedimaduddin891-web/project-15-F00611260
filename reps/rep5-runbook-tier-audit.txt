Reflection draft — paraphrase this

The rotate-logs step requires approval because deleting journal records is
irreversible even though its blast radius is limited to one host. The
clear-apt-cache step is gated in my version because it changes state and could
slow an emergency package reinstall or rollback; a human can approve it after
checking the incident context. The scale-volume step is gated because it
creates continuing cost and is difficult to reverse. For each action I asked:
"Is it reversible?" and "What is its blast radius across availability, data,
and cost?"
